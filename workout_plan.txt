WORKOUT PLAN - SPRINT/JUMP POWER, MOBILITY, STABILITY
Soviet/Russian + Japanese Training Methods + AthleanX/Squat U
+ Knees Over Toes knee/tibialis work
=================================================================

COACHING PHILOSOPHY
-------------------
1. GPP before SPP - General Physical Preparedness (joint health, tissue
   resilience, movement quality) comes before chasing strength/power
   numbers, even with full gym access.

2. Tendons adapt slower than muscle and train differently. Build tendon
   stiffness through controlled isometrics/eccentrics before ballistic
   or plyometric work. Loading a weak tendon with jumps before it's
   prepped causes tendinopathy, not springiness.

3. Every session is corrective - mobility isn't a separate day, it's
   built into the warm-up and accessory work of every session.

4. Unilateral loading over bilateral to fix imbalance - bilateral
   barbell work lets the strong side compensate and hides the problem.

5. Fix the weak link before loading the pattern - scapular winging and
   trap weakness get fixed with low-load activation work before heavy
   pulling volume is added.

6. Low axial spinal load, high output - sprinters/jumpers need force
   production, not spinal compression. Trap bar, single-leg, and hip
   thrust patterns replace back squat/conventional deadlift where
   possible.

7. Pelvic obliquity correction (right hip higher, left hip flexor
   stiff) - lengthen the tight/overactive side (left hip flexor) AND
   independently strengthen the weak/underactive side (right glute
   med) at the same time. Stretching alone won't hold; a compensating
   muscle will just retighten without direct strength work on the
   weak side.

8. Plyometrics held back until week 2-3, given the ankle and pelvic
   issues - reactive load on a stiff ankle and unlevel pelvis is how
   you get strains. Week 1-2 is pure strength/stability/mobility
   density.

9. Long holds over short holds. Tightness this chronic does not
   change under 30-second stretches. Every stretch and isometric
   runs 45-120s, scaled by drill: 45-60s for activation and small
   muscles, 90-120s for the stiff tissue being lengthened (left hip
   flexor, left calf). The tight side always gets the longer hold.

10. Knee and tibialis work is not accessory. A stiff ankle that
    can't dorsiflex and a knee that never loads through full flexion
    both feed the same compensation pattern. Tibialis raises, full-
    range knee flexion, and eccentric knee extension appear on every
    lower day.

11. No sprinting. Force production comes from heavy sled push/pull
    and triple-extension work, not from top-end sprint mechanics.

EQUIPMENT AVAILABLE ON HAND
=================================================================
- Weighted vest
- Stretch rope with loops
- Heavy Reebok resistance band (red)
- Medium Reebok resistance band (black)
- 5 colored resistance bands with hooks, 10-50 lb
  (handles and ankle wraps included)
- 3 elevated boards (1 medium height, 2 low height)
- Ab roller

Gym access is assumed for barbell, trap bar, dumbbell, kettlebell,
landmine and sled work. Everything in the NIGHTLY block runs on the
on-hand equipment above, at home, with nothing else needed.

=================================================================
WEEKS 1-2 RAMP-IN (READ FIRST)
Coaching notes only - NOT part of the app seed. The day templates
below are the steady-state prescription; run these substitutions
for the first two weeks, then run the plan as written from week 3.
=================================================================
The philosophy says tendons get isometric/eccentric prep before
ballistic load (points 2 and 8). A loaded jump shrug and a depth
drop ARE ballistic work, so for weeks 1-2:

- Trap bar deadlift jump-shrug (Day 1, Day 3) -> trap bar deadlift
  with a fast, aggressive concentric but NO jump, same 5x4. The
  intent stays triple-extension speed; the landing impact waits.
- Depth drop into stick landing (Day 3) -> step-down from the low
  board into a stick landing (step, don't drop), same 4x4.
- KB swings stay as written - low-impact hinge, acceptable from
  week 1.
- Nordic hamstring curl: start at 2x3, not 4x5. Nordics from a
  cold start cause multi-day soreness in nearly everyone. Add a
  set or a rep per week toward the written 4x5 by week 3-4.
- Copenhagen plank: short-lever (knee-supported) variant is the
  starting point. Earn the full-lever version by holding a clean
  45s short-lever first; progress lever before time.

From week 3, everything above reverts to the plan as written,
provided nothing is producing joint pain or next-day tendon
soreness. If it is, stay on the ramp-in version another week.

=================================================================
RE-CHECK EVERY 4 WEEKS
Coaching notes only - NOT part of the app seed.
=================================================================
The plan exists to change measurable things. Every 4 weeks,
re-test and write the numbers down:

1. Knee-to-wall test, each side (ankle dorsiflexion): toes a
   measured distance from the wall, knee touches without the heel
   lifting. Track max distance per side. Left should be closing
   on right.
2. Standing pelvic level check: hands on iliac crests in a
   mirror, or a photo from behind. Is the right crest still
   sitting higher?
3. Couch stretch time-to-discomfort, each side: seconds until
   tension turns to discomfort. Left climbing toward right is the
   win condition.

If none of the three has moved after 8 weeks, the program needs
changing - more of the same is not the answer.

=================================================================
WEEKLY STRUCTURE
=================================================================
Day 1: Sprint mechanics + hip flexor strength + left ankle
Day 2: Shoulder abductors + scapular control + core anti-tilt
Day 3: Reactive prep (sub-max, no true plyo yet) + posterior chain
Day 4: Hip abductors/extensors + pelvic correction + glute strength
Nightly: Non-negotiable bedtime drills (every night, ~20 min)

Session length target: 85-115 min including warm-up (shortened from
90-120 when rest periods were cut on 2026-08-23).
Rest periods are prescribed per block and are part of the session,
not optional padding.

Rest structure (updated 2026-08-23): the bracketed rest runs between
rounds inside a block. At the END of each category (warm-up, knee
block, superset A, superset B, ...) the app runs a single MAIN REST
instead of resting after that category final round - one longer
break at each category change rather than two back to back.

Recommended spacing: Day 1 / Day 2 / rest / Day 3 / Day 4 / rest /
rest (e.g. Mon-Tue-Thu-Fri). Day 2 is the buffer between the two
heavy lower days - Days 1 and 3 back-to-back stacks jump-shrug,
sled and Nordic stress on the same tissue inside 24 hours.

Block order within every day (updated 2026-08-22):
1. Warm-up
2. Knee/tendon block - moved BEFORE the main lifts as targeted
   knee prep (KOT-style: sled drag and tibialis work prime the
   knee before it gets loaded).
3. Explosive pairs stay first among the main lifts - ballistic
   work on fatigued tendons at the end of a session is exactly
   what philosophy points 2 and 8 forbid.
4. Unilateral before bilateral everywhere else - the weak side
   works while fresh, the strong side cannot compensate, and
   within each superset pair the single-leg/single-arm drill
   goes first.

DAY 1 - SPRINT MECHANICS + HIP FLEXOR STRENGTH + LEFT ANKLE
Target duration: 100-112 min
=================================================================

WARM-UP (targets: ankle complex, hip flexors bilaterally, glute med,
calves, tibialis)  [~18 min, rest 20s between drills]
- Couch stretch: left 120s / right 60s
- Banded ankle dorsiflexion rocks: 15/side (left first)
- Tibialis raise, back to wall: 3x20 (left extra set: 3 left / 2 right
  if done single-leg)
- Banded hip flexor march-in-place (posterior-lateral pull): 15/side
- Lateral band walks: 20 steps/direction
- Single-leg ankle pogo hops: 15/side
- Leg swings front/back + lateral: 12/side
- Hip flexor activation - standing banded marches: 12/side

KNEE / TIBIALIS BLOCK (knee prep - runs BEFORE the supersets)
[rest 45s between sets, ~14 min]
- Backward sled drag (light-moderate, continuous): 3x30m
- Tibialis raise, loaded (band + ankle wrap or plate on toes):
  3x20
- ATG split squat, front foot on low board, left-biased:
  4x8 left / 3x8 right

MAIN - SUPERSET A  [rest 90s between rounds, ~15 min]
1a. Trap bar deadlift jump-shrug: 5x4
1b. Weighted hanging knee raise / hip flexor strength (add ankle
    weight or band resistance): 4x10

MAIN - SUPERSET B  [rest 60s between rounds, ~14 min]
2a. Single-leg box step-up, left-biased: 4x8 left / 3x8 right
2b. Standing banded hip flexion (resisted knee drive), left extra
    set: 4x12 left / 3x12 right

MAIN - SUPERSET C  [rest 60s between rounds, ~16 min]
3a. Copenhagen plank (adductor/pelvic stability), left extra:
    4x60s left / 3x45s right
3b. KB swings: 4x15

POWER / FORCE PRODUCTION  [rest 120s between efforts, ~15 min]
- Heavy sled push: 6x20m
  Load heavy enough that stride shortens and the push is grinding,
  not running. No sprinting.

TENDON FINISHER  [~10 min]
- Single-leg ankle pogo hops (left extra set): 3x15
- Wall sit iso hold: 2x90s
- Standing calf raise iso hold at top: 2x45s

=================================================================
DAY 2 - SHOULDER ABDUCTORS + SCAPULAR CONTROL + CORE ANTI-TILT
Target duration: 82-95 min
=================================================================

WARM-UP (targets: rotator cuff, serratus, scap stabilizers,
obliques/QL)  [~15 min, rest 20s between drills]
- Band pull-aparts: 20
- Band external rotation at 90-degree abduction: 15/side
- Wall slides with scapular protraction: 12
- Prone Y-T-W (bodyweight): 10 each
- Doorway pec stretch: 90s/side
- Side plank hip dips (QL activation), left extra set: 3x8 left /
  2x8 right
- Arm circles + cross-body swings: 12 each

KNEE / TIBIALIS MAINTENANCE (runs BEFORE the supersets)
[rest 45s between sets, ~12 min]
- Reverse Nordic (kneeling, 4-count lower to tolerance): 3x8
- Tibialis raise, back to wall: 3x25
- Standing calf stretch, knee bent (soleus): 90s left / 60s right

MAIN - SUPERSET A  [rest 60s between rounds, ~15 min]
1a. Half-kneeling single-arm band row, left extra set:
    4x12 left / 3x12 right
1b. Landmine press single arm, left-biased: 4x8 left / 3x8 right

MAIN - SUPERSET B  [rest 60s between rounds, ~13 min]
2a. Serratus punch (banded): 4x12/side
2b. Prone trap raise (light DB): 4x12

MAIN - SUPERSET C  [rest 60s between rounds, ~13 min]
3a. DB scaption raise: 4x12
3b. Face pulls with external rotation emphasis: 4x15

CORE / PELVIS  [rest 45s between sets, ~14 min]
- Side plank with abduction (right side extra to fire underactive
  glute med): 3x10 reps/side
- Pallof press: 3x12/side
- Pallof press iso hold: 3x60s/side
- Ab roller from knees, slow: 3x8

DAY 3 - REACTIVE PREP (SUB-MAX, NO TRUE PLYO YET) + POSTERIOR CHAIN
Target duration: 86-98 min
=================================================================

WARM-UP (targets: posterior chain, calves, anti-rotation core, hip

- Pallof press: 12/side
- Banded hip flexor march: 12/side

- Broad jump hold-and-stick (sub-max, no rebound): x6
- Depth march (low board, stick landing): x6
- Band-resisted lateral bounds (controlled): 10/side
- Ankle pogo hops: 20

KNEE / HAMSTRING BLOCK (knee prep - runs BEFORE the supersets)
[rest 60s between sets, ~15 min]
- Backward sled drag (moderate, continuous): 3x30m
- ATG split squat, front foot on low board, left-biased:
  3x8 left / 2x8 right
- Nordic hamstring curl, 4-count eccentric (rope-assisted as
  needed): 4x5

MAIN - SUPERSET A  [rest 90s between rounds, ~16 min]
1a. Trap bar jump shrug: 5x4
1b. Single-leg RDL (KB), left-biased: 4x10 left / 3x10 right

MAIN - SUPERSET B  [rest 60s between rounds, ~14 min]
2a. Depth drop into stick landing (low board, control-focused): 4x4

MAIN - SUPERSET C  [rest 90s between rounds, ~16 min]
3a. Suitcase carry, left-loaded: 3x30m/side

DAY 4 - HIP ABDUCTORS/EXTENSORS + PELVIC CORRECTION + GLUTE STRENGTH

KNEE / TIBIALIS BLOCK (knee prep - runs BEFORE the supersets)
[rest 45s between sets, ~14 min]
- Banded leg curl (ankle wrap), left-biased: 4x15 left / 3x15 right
- Reverse Nordic (4-count lower): 3x8
- ATG split squat, front foot on low board, left-biased:
  3x8 left / 2x8 right

MAIN - SUPERSET A  [rest 60s between rounds, ~16 min]
1a. Single-leg RDL (DB), left-biased: 4x8 left / 3x8 right
1b. Copenhagen plank, left extra: 4x60s left / 3x45s right

MAIN - SUPERSET B  [rest 90s between rounds, ~16 min]
2a. Standing banded hip flexion, left-biased: 4x12 left / 3x12 right
2b. Barbell hip thrust: 5x8

MAIN - SUPERSET C  [rest 60s between rounds, ~13 min]
3a. Right-side single-leg glute bridge (correcting right glute med

- Standing banded hip abduction iso hold, right extra:

=================================================================
NIGHTLY NON-NEGOTIABLE (EVERY NIGHT, ~20 MIN)
=================================================================
Low-intensity and control-focused only - nothing ballistic or high
heart rate before bed. Purpose: tissue quality, activation patterning,
reinforcing the pelvic correction while calm - not adding training
fatigue. Holds here stay in the 45-60s band; the 90-120s work belongs
in the lifting sessions, not before sleep. One deliberate exception:
the left calf stretches run 90s (60s + 30s extra), because the left
calf is the stiff tissue being lengthened.

Runs entirely on on-hand equipment: bands, stretch rope, low board.

CALVES / ANKLE / TIBIALIS (targets left ankle stiffness)
- Wall calf stretch (knee straight): 60s/side, left extra 30s
- Wall calf stretch (knee bent, soleus): 60s/side, left extra 30s
- Slow controlled calf raises off a low board, full range: 2x15
  (2 count up, 3 count down, no bounce)
- Tibialis raise, back to wall: 2x20

GLUTES (targets right glute med underactivity + left glute rarely
firing)
- Glute bridge iso hold: 3x45s (squeeze at top, both sides working)
- Single-leg glute bridge, right-biased: 2x8 right / 2x8 left
- Banded clamshells, slow tempo, left extra set: 15/side

CORE / PELVIS (anti-tilt, reinforces hip correction)
- Dead bug, slow controlled: 2x8/side
- Side plank hold (modified on knees if needed), right extra 15s:
  2x45s/side
- 90/90 hip switch flow: 8 reps, ending in held stretch each side 45s

CLOSE EVERY NIGHT WITH:
- Couch stretch: left 120s / right 60s
  This is the one drill that directly targets the stiff left hip
  flexor and should never be skipped, lifting day or not.

Note: nothing here should cause next-day soreness. If a movement
causes soreness, drop the reps or shorten the hold, not the drill.
Consistency matters more than intensity for shifting the pelvic
obliquity over weeks.
